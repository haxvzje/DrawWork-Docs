# Toggle Commands

Turn on/off Draw functions!

### /toggle snipe

The `/toggle snipe` command allows you to enable or disable the ***Draw Sniper™*** feature on your account. This feature can be turned on or off based on your preferences, affecting how certain actions are managed in your account.

### /toggle antiscam

The `/toggle antiscam` command allows you to enable or disable the ***Draw AntiScam™*** feature on your server. This feature is designed to help protect your server from scam link. You must have administrative permissions to use this command.
